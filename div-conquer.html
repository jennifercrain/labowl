Divide and Conquer Debugging

Help students break a debugging problem into smaller pieces they can test one at a time.

Isolate One Piece at a Time

Programming problems can feel overwhelming when students try to fix everything at once. Divide and Conquer debugging helps students isolate smaller pieces of the code so they can test them individually.

You Might Ask

  • “What’s the smallest section we could test right now?”
  • “What happens if we temporarily comment out everything except this part?”
  • “What part of the program controls the output we’re seeing?”

By narrowing the scope of the investigation, students can focus on one variable or section at a time. This strategy works well during the Break It Down stage of Lab Owl.
