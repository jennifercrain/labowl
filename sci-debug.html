Debugging as an Experiment

Scientific debugging treats coding problems like an experiment. The student forms a hypothesis, tests it, and observes the result. In a session, the Peer Navigator helps the student make predictions before running the code.

You Might Ask

  • “What do you think is causing this behavior?”
  • “If we change this variable, what do you expect to happen?”
  • “What result would confirm that idea?”

Predicting the result makes the test intentional and gives the student something specific to look for. This strategy fits especially well during Work the Hypothesis in the Lab Owl process.
