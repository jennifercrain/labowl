The Lab Owl Framework

To support Programming Lab sessions, we use a framework called Lab Owl. Lab Owl serves as a structured coaching guide that helps Peer Navigators facilitate debugging conversations without stepping into tutoring or providing content instruction.

Programming problems often feel overwhelming to students because they do not know where to start. Lab Owl gives both you and the student a clear direction for the conversation. It helps sessions stay structured, student-driven, focused on skill development, and aligned with our mission. When you follow the Lab Owl process, you help students move from confusion to clarity while building stronger debugging habits.

Lab Owl helps students slow down so they can clearly observe what is happening and make intentional decisions about what to test next.

Lab Owl Helps Students

  • Identify where a problem begins.
  • Observe what their program is doing.
  • Isolate variables to test.
  • Recognize patterns in outputs.
  • Build independent debugging habits.
